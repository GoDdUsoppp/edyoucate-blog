Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers about NIOS Open Schooling and UGC-DEB recognized Distance Learning programs.

Anyone aged 14+ can enroll for Class 10th, and 10th-passed students (or those transferring from CBSE/GSEB/ICSE) can join Class 12th. NIOS is an autonomous National Board under the Ministry of Education, Govt. of India. Its certificates are completely valid for NEET, JEE, UPSC, university admissions, and all government & private jobs.
Yes! Under NIOS Stream 2 and On-Demand Examination (ODE), students who did not pass in CBSE, ICSE, or State Boards can transfer marks of up to 2 passed subjects (TOC) and appear only for the remaining subjects to save their academic year without delay.
Yes, 100%. All university programs we guide students toward (B.A., B.Com, BBA, BCA, MBA, MCA, M.Com, MA) are UGC-DEB approved and AICTE recognized. As per UGC gazette notifications, online and distance degrees carry equal legal validity as on-campus regular degrees.
Absolutely. Our programs provide digital learning portals, recorded lectures, weekend doubt-clearing sessions, and local examination options, enabling working adults to earn accredited degrees at their own pace without quitting their jobs.
